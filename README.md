# FIN 4453 Midterm II Trainer

Practice site for FIN 4453 (Financial Modeling & Analytics) Midterm II on Module 3, capital budgeting.

**Open it:** https://w-ducdoan.github.io/fin4453-trainer/

- **Lessons:** 12 lessons in slide order: NPV, IRR, NPV profiles and mutually exclusive projects, multiple IRRs, operating cash flow, after-tax salvage, unequal lives, mid-year discounting, free cash flow, WACC and terminal value, the equity bridge, and sensitivity tables.
- **Drills:** select-all questions with fresh numbers each time, scored like the exam: 2 × (correct ticked ÷ correct total), −1 per wrong tick, floor 0. Every statement is explained after you check. Also includes written scenario practice with model answers and key points.
- **Practice exam:** the course's practice midterm with a worked key. The key was written for this site, not by the instructor.
- **Mock exam:** 75 minutes, 25 points, or the practice midterm under a timer.
- **Review sheet:** every rule and formula to memorize for a closed-book exam.

Progress saves in your browser. Written answers are self-graded against the key points.

`src/` holds the source. Run `python src/build.py` to rebuild `index.html`.
